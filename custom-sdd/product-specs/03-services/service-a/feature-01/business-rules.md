# Business Rules: Customer Registration

- **BR-REG-001:** Email comparison is case-insensitive.
- **BR-REG-002:** A customer ID uses the `cus-` prefix and is never reused.
- **BR-REG-003:** A profile is not created when validation fails.
- **BR-REG-004:** Registration requires `customer:create` permission.

These rules are dummy data for `SVC-A-F01`.
